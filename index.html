Marlon Etheredge MSc

Hello, I'm Marlon Etheredge. I am a PhD researcher in the Distributed and Parallel Systems Group at the University of Innsbruck. My research focuses on programming paradigms, runtime systems, and optimization techniques for the Cloud-Edge-IoT continuum. Read more about my research in my publications and blog.
